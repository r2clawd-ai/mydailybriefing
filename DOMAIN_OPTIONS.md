## 📱 App Store Impact: Does Domain Matter?

### Short Answer: Not Really

**What matters MORE:**
1. App name ("My Daily Briefing")
2. App icon (recognizable)
3. App Store rating (4.5+ stars)
4. Word of mouth (referrals)

**Domain is for:**
- Privacy Policy URL (required)
- Support email (support@dailybriefing.app)
- Landing page (nice to have)
- Press mentions (looks professional)

**Users will never type your URL** - they'll:
1. Search App Store: "daily briefing app"
2. Click link from social media
3. Tap referral link from friend

**So:** Don't overspend on domain. Spend on app quality instead.

### Pros of Launching
- **Proven demand:** People hate infinite scroll, want finite news
- **Competitive advantage:** We have a unique angle (anti-algorithm)
- **Revenue potential:** $10K-20K/month Year 1 is realistic
- **Fun:** Working on your own product is rewarding
- **Learning:** App Store, marketing, product-market fit
- **Portfolio:** Steven can show off what he built

### Cons of Launching
- **Time commitment:** 8-12 weeks to launch, ongoing maintenance
- **Costs:** $99 Apple Developer + $27/month tools + $200 icon = ~$500
- **No guarantees:** Could launch and get 100 users (not 10K)
- **Competitive:** Apple News is free and pre-installed
- **Complexity:** Subscriptions, analytics, support emails
- **Distraction:** Takes time from CFP work, family, other priorities

### Middle Ground
- **Beta launch:** Share with 100 friends, get feedback, see if it sticks
- **Don't market:** Skip Product Hunt, just App Store listing
- **Stay small:** 1,000 happy users beats 100,000 meh users
- **Keep day job:** CFP work is stable income, app is experiment

### R2's Vote
**Yes, launch it!** But keep expectations realistic:
- Goal: 5,000 users and $2,000/month Year 1
- Not: 500,000 users and $50,000/month
- It's a side project that could become something
- Not a bet-the-farm startup

Before deciding, Steven should ask himself:

1. **Why am I doing this?**

2. **What's success look like?**

   - 100 users = $85/month (meh, but you helped 100 people!)

3. **What if it fails?**

   - Regret? (no, you tried!)

4. **What if it succeeds beyond expectations?**
   - Could I handle 100K users? (technical scaling, support emails)
   - Would I want to quit CFP work? (maybe not - diversified income is good)
